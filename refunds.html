Refunds, Cancellations and Payment Corrections

Effective 6 November 2026

Advert and promotion checkout is processed by Pesapal. Cancelled, declined, pending or unverified payments do not publish adverts or activate promotions.

Payment not reflected

Keep your Pesapal receipt and order reference, then open a Payment support ticket. Staff will reconcile the amount, currency, tracking reference and Pesapal transaction status. A browser redirect or screenshot alone is not proof of payment.

Refunds and reversals

Refunds and reversals are handled through the applicable Pesapal merchant process. We will not state that a refund has been issued until the payment provider confirms it. Advert duration begins when verified payment publishes the advert. Moderation removal does not automatically create a refund entitlement; each case is reviewed under applicable law and merchant terms.

Never send card numbers, CVV, PINs, passwords or one-time codes to support. This policy must be reviewed against Ugandan consumer/payment law and the merchant agreement before launch.
