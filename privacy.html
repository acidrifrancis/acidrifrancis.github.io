Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Northern Uganda Market Connect processes account details (name, email and phone), adverts and images, buyer/seller messages, reports, support and dispute records, payment references/status from Pesapal, and technical security records. We use this data to provide accounts, display adverts, route messages, verify payments, prevent abuse, moderate content and resolve support cases.

Supabase provides authentication/database/storage, Render hosts the application and Pesapal processes checkout. The marketplace stores payment references and verification results, not full card credentials. Public adverts are visible to visitors; private messages and support records are restricted to participants and authorised staff.
