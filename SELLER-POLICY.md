# Seller Policy

- Seller registration, login and dashboard access are free.
- Sellers pay only when they submit an advert for publication. The selected duration and total UGX checkout amount must be shown before redirecting to Pesapal.
- An advert is public only after server-side verification of the Pesapal transaction, matching order reference, tracking ID, amount and currency.
- Sellers must own or be authorised to offer the advertised goods/services and must provide accurate descriptions, location and contact details.
- No illegal, counterfeit, stolen, dangerous or misleading offers, spam, impersonation or discriminatory/harassing content.
- Advert duration starts when the advert is published and ends at its expiry timestamp.
- Promotion bids are paid submissions. A paid bid is not a guarantee of approval or a particular number of views. Approved promotion dates are controlled by the marketplace.
- The marketplace may reject, suspend or remove adverts and suspend accounts after reports or policy review.
- Sellers should respond to buyer enquiries promptly and never request passwords or one-time security codes.
