# Buyer Policy

- Buyers should register or log in before messaging a seller. A message is routed only to the seller of the selected advert.
- Check product condition, authenticity, seller identity, location, delivery arrangements and total price before making a purchase.
- Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary personal documents to sellers.
- The marketplace does not hold buyer funds or guarantee an off-platform transaction unless a specific protected-payment feature is expressly introduced.
- Report suspicious adverts, impersonation, harassment or prohibited items using the report feature or support.
- Keep receipts, order references, messages and delivery evidence if a transaction dispute arises.
