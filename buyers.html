Buyer Policy

  • Register or log in before contacting a seller. Messages are tied to the selected advert and delivered to that advert's seller.
  • Verify item condition, authenticity, seller identity, location, price and delivery arrangements before paying.
  • Never share passwords, verification codes or full card details with sellers.
  • Report suspicious, prohibited or misleading adverts.
  • The marketplace does not hold buyer funds or guarantee off-platform transactions.
  • Use support/disputes for marketplace-related complaints and include relevant advert/order references without sharing sensitive financial details.
