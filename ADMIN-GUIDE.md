# Administrator quick guide

## First administrator
Register your own account on the website, then promote that account using the SQL statement in the main README. Log out and back in to refresh the role in the PHP session.

## Administrator dashboard
- **Adverts:** review all adverts; approve, reject or suspend.
- **Reports:** review user-submitted complaints; mark reviewed or dismissed.
- **Users:** see registered users and promote/demote administrators. Do not demote your only administrator.
- **Pricing:** add and edit advert durations and UGX prices; disable options that should no longer be sold.
- **Categories:** add, rename and deactivate listing categories.
- **Payments:** inspect Pesapal references, amounts, seller and status.
- **Fees & settings:** configure fixed and/or percentage fee pass-through charged to the seller.
- **Payment test:** check Supabase access and Pesapal token authentication.

## Important
Keep at least two trusted administrators. Never share login credentials. Only grant administrator access to people who need to moderate content, view payment information or change marketplace settings.
