# Customer Support and Disputes

Use the website support route to report login/account problems, advert publication delays, suspected fraud, payment reconciliation issues, prohibited listings or buyer/seller disputes.

Include the account email, advert ID or payment order reference, date/time and a concise description. Never send passwords, card numbers, Supabase keys or Pesapal secrets.

Support staff should acknowledge a case, verify the requester, preserve relevant messages/payment references, record actions in the admin audit log, and update the ticket status. Payment disputes must be checked against Pesapal's transaction-status response; a browser return page or screenshot alone is not proof of payment.

A dispute review can facilitate communication and record a decision. It does not make the marketplace the seller or guarantee recovery of funds for an off-platform sale.
