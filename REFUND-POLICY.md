# Refund, cancellation and payment correction policy

**Effective 6 November 2026**

Advert and promotion payments are processed through Pesapal. A checkout that is cancelled, declined, pending or not verified does not publish an advert or activate a promotion.

If a payment appears successful in Pesapal but the marketplace does not update:
1. Keep the Pesapal receipt and merchant/order reference.
2. Open a support ticket under **Payment** and include the order reference, amount, currency and approximate time.
3. Marketplace staff will reconcile the record against Pesapal's transaction-status API and IPN records.
4. Any refund or reversal is processed only through the merchant/Pesapal procedure applicable to the transaction. The marketplace will not claim that a refund has been issued until the provider confirms it.

Advert duration starts when the verified payment publishes the advert. A later moderation suspension or removal for a policy violation does not automatically mean a refund is due; cases are reviewed individually and subject to applicable law and Pesapal merchant terms.

No card number, CVV, PIN, password or one-time code should be sent to support.

Before public launch, confirm the refund position with Pesapal and have this policy reviewed for applicable Ugandan consumer and payment law.
