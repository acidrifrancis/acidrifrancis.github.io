# Privacy Policy — Northern Uganda Market Connect

**Effective date:** 6 November 2026

Northern Uganda Market Connect ("the marketplace", "we") connects buyers and sellers in Uganda. This policy explains the personal information processed when you use the service.

## Information we process
We process account details you submit (name, email, phone number and password credential managed by Supabase Auth), adverts and uploaded images, approximate business location/district, messages, reports, support requests, dispute information, payment references and status returned by Pesapal, and technical/security logs such as request timestamps and rate-limit identifiers.

Do not include bank-card numbers, passwords, national identification numbers or other highly sensitive information in adverts, messages or support tickets.

## How we use information
We use information to create and secure accounts, publish and moderate adverts, route buyer messages to the relevant seller, process and verify Pesapal payments, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, provide support, maintain records and improve the service.

## Sharing
We share the minimum information required with service providers that operate the marketplace, including Supabase (authentication/database/storage), Render (hosting) and Pesapal (payment processing). Public adverts and their images are visible to visitors. Private messages, support conversations and account details are not intended to be public. We do not sell personal information.

## Security and retention
We use access controls, server-side payment verification and security monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate recordkeeping requirements, resolve disputes and prevent fraud. Payment and audit records may be retained after account closure where necessary.

## Your choices
You may request access, correction or deletion of your account information by contacting marketplace support. Some transaction, fraud-prevention or legal records may need to be retained. We may ask you to verify account ownership before acting.

## Children
The marketplace is intended for adults and business users. Do not create an account for a child.

## Changes and contact
We may update this policy when the service changes. The latest published version will be shown on the website. For privacy questions, use the website's support/contact route.
